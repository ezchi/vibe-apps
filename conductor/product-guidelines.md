# Product Guidelines: Vibe-Apps

## Design Philosophy: "Modern Vibe"
Our apps balance professional utility with a cutting-edge, experimental aesthetic. We embrace the "vibe-coding" spirit by being transparent about our evolution and prioritizing high-quality "feel."

## Tone & Voice
- **Minimalist & Professional:** Use clean, concise, and direct language. Focus on utility and efficiency. Avoid unnecessary fluff, but maintain a helpful and sophisticated tone.

## Visual Identity
- **Glassmorphism:** Utilize transparency, background blurs, and subtle borders to create a layered, futuristic depth.
- **Modern Web Aesthetic:** Incorporate vibrant gradients and soft shadows.
- **High-Quality Micro-interactions:** Every action should provide smooth visual feedback to enhance the "vibe."

## User Experience (UX)
- **Mobile-First Responsiveness:** Design for the smallest touchscreens (like the Pixel Fold in its closed or open states) first. Desktop layouts (macOS) should be an intentional expansion of the mobile experience.
- **Platform Agnostic (Mostly):** Maintain a consistent look and feel across Android, macOS, and Web to create a unified brand identity.

## LLM Interaction Guidelines
- **Total Transparency:** Every response must be clearly labeled with its source model (e.g., GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 Pro).
- **Metadata Visibility:** Display performance metrics like latency or token counts where helpful for power users to compare efficiency alongside accuracy.

## Development Culture
- **Community-Driven Documentation:** We document not just *what* we built, but the "vibes" and reasoning behind our decisions, fostering an open and experimental project atmosphere.
